Color Palette

Seven colors carry the entire system — warm, earthy, and confident. Each is paired with the semantic roles it plays across the Kasa interface.

Brand

The signature warmth of Kasa: amber leads, cream grounds.

Amber

HEX #E9901E

Usestext-brand-primary, surface-brand-primary, logo wordmark, CTA button fill (“Reserve a table”), nav active link, section accent text, price text on menu cards, footer logo.

Cream

HEX #F5F0E8

Usessurface-primary (main page background), card background on the menu-items section.

Secondary

Grounding depth and contrast for heroes, footers, and key moments.

Forest Green

HEX #0C3D2E

Usessurface-hero (hero background), surface-footer-bottom (bottom footer band), navbar background, “Our story” button fill, surface-inverse for text on light backgrounds.

Crimson

HEX #BE1737

Usessurface-brand-secondary, “Find us” section background, surface-cta-band (“Ready to dine with us?” strip).

Navy

HEX #004982

Usessurface-map-placeholder (map block in the Find Us section), surface-accent (secondary interactive elements).

Neutral

The structural backbone — text, dark surfaces, and clean inverse.

Charcoal

HEX #1E1E1A

Usestext-primary (body text, card titles, prices), navbar background (slightly transparent overlay), surface-footer-base (main footer background).

White

HEX #FFFFFF

Usestext-inverse (text on dark/colored backgrounds), hero headline text, “Find us” section text, footer body text, nav links on the dark navbar, outlined button (“View menu”) text and border.

Contrast Compliance

All text-on-background combinations in this palette meet WCAG AA contrast requirements:

  • White on Forest10.8:1✓ Pass
  • White on Crimson5.1:1✓ Pass
  • White on Charcoal16.1:1✓ Pass
  • Charcoal on Cream14.2:1✓ Pass
  • Charcoal on Amber3.1:1— Large text / headings only

Avoid placing small body text in Charcoal directly on Amber.
