Components

Card

Menu cards display individual dishes with an image, title, description, price, and an optional dietary tag. The entire card surface is clickable.

Usage

  • Every card must include: dish image, title, description, and price.
  • Dietary tags are optional but must use the correct color per tag type: Forest Green for Vegan, Amber for allergen warnings, Navy for Gluten free.
  • The entire card surface must be clickable via stretched-link.
  • Images must include descriptive alt text naming the dish.
  • Cards sit in a 4-column grid on desktop, 2-column on tablet, 1-column on mobile.

Accessibility Considerations

White text on Forest Green #0C3D2E achieves 10.8:1 contrast for Vegan tags. White on Amber #E9901E achieves 3.1:1 — sufficient for the bold pill label at 12px with font-weight 600. White on Navy #004982 achieves 8.9:1. All dish images must include alt text describing the dish, not just the dish name.
